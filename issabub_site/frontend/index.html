OUR COMPANY

Issabub Nigeria Limited is an Ogun-based general construction and civil engineering firm providing comprehensive project execution for residential, commercial, industrial and infrastructure clients across Nigeria.

Founded on January 28, 2014, the company operates from the Ijoko Ota corridor and manages building and civil engineering works from initial site clearance through final handover.
